# TAJO — AGENTS.md

## 1. PROJECT PURPOSE

TAJO is a modern technology partner for service businesses.

Core positioning:

> Intelligent infrastructure for the space between inquiry and booking.

TAJO helps service businesses capture, respond to, and follow up with customer
opportunities using the right combination of:

- websites
- automation
- integrations
- AI
- intelligent workflows
- existing business software

TAJO does not sell technology for its own sake.

The goal is to help a service business lose fewer good opportunities between
the moment someone becomes interested and the moment they become a customer.

The website must communicate this clearly through both copy AND interaction.

---

# 2. WHAT TAJO IS NOT

Do not accidentally position TAJO as:

- a SaaS product
- an AI agency
- a traditional consultancy
- a generic web design agency
- a lead-generation company
- a CRM replacement
- an automation tool marketplace

TAJO may build websites, automation and AI systems, but these are means to
solve the underlying business problem.

The central idea is:

> Someone became interested. What happened next?

TAJO finds what is missing and builds or fixes it.

---

# 3. CORE CUSTOMER JOURNEY

The conceptual TAJO journey is:

CAPTURE
↓
RESPOND
↓
FOLLOW UP
↓
BOOKED JOB

These are not three separate TAJO products.

They represent three important moments in the customer's journey.

Whenever possible, the website should demonstrate this journey rather than
simply describing it.

Example:

Customer:
"Hi, I need an estimate for replacing my roof."

↓

TAJO captures the inquiry.

↓

Useful information is identified.

↓

The customer receives an appropriate response.

↓

The opportunity is routed to the business.

↓

The customer does not book.

↓

TAJO initiates or schedules the appropriate follow-up.

↓

The opportunity remains active.

This type of behaviour should be used as inspiration for interactive website
experiences.
